- VPNs allows to send traffic securely over an insecure medium 
- Private information can be sent securely without interception
- Traffic is encrypted so that confidentiality is maintained
- Was mainly developed for protocols which were non-encrypted like - FTP, Telnet, SMTP, HTTP, SNMP v1
- Asymmetric algorithm:
    - Different keys are used for encryption and decryption. Ex. Public key algorithms
    - Something encrypted with your private key can only be decrypted with your public key
    - Something encrypted with your public key can only be decrypted with your private key
    - A public key must be generated from a private key
- Symmetric algorithm:
    - Same key is used for encryption and decryption. Ex. Secret key algorithms
    - Recommended algorithm is AES for VPNs for corporate environment

- Significance of using VPNs:
    - Data confidentiality  
        - No one else should be able to read the information by manipulating the Data
        - Provided by data encryption and keys
    - Data integrity:
        - Know that the data has traversed unchanged between the two parties
    - Data Origin Authentication:
        - The receiver can verify that the protected data could only have originated from the sender
    - Antireplay protection:
        - Verify that each packet is unique and not duplicated


IPSec (IP Security):
    - Network layer protocol (suite of protocols)
    - Protects and authenticates IP packets
    - Framework of open standards that is algorithm independent
    - Protocols includes - ESP or AH or ESP + AH
    - Encryption - DES or 3DES or AES
    - Authentication - MD5 or SHA
    - DH - DH1 or DH2 or DH5
